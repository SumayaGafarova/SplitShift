# SplitShift — Staff-First Presentation & Walkthrough Guide 🚀

> **Target Audience:** Frontline staff, baristas, waitstaff, kitchen prep, and cashiers (not corporate leads or executives).  
> **Core Message:** *"Shift swaps without the group chat chaos, awkward begging, or waiting 6 hours for an answer."*

---

## 🎯 1. Why Presenting to Staff First is the Winning Strategy
Frontline workers are the everyday users whose lives are directly impacted by shift scheduling:
- If staff don't love using the tool, adoption will fail regardless of what management buys.
- Frontline workers fear awkward favors, guilt over calling in sick, missed hours/pay, and chaotic WhatsApp groups where messages get lost.
- When workers see that SplitShift gives **them** control over their hours, flexibility, and immediate visibility into who can cover them, they become internal champions for the platform.

## 🎤 3. The 5-Minute Staff Presentation Script

When gathering your cafe/store team together (e.g. during a 5-minute pre-shift huddle or lunch break), follow this pitch structure:

### Step 1: Hook the Pain (The WhatsApp Nightmare)
> *"Raise your hand if you’ve ever had an urgent exam, family event, or doctor's appointment, posted in our team WhatsApp chat asking 'Can anyone cover my Friday night?', and were left on 'Read' by 12 people?"*
> 
> *"Or worse — you didn't know who was free, had to text 5 different people privately, and then still had to chase down the shift manager to ask if it was okay?"*

### Step 2: Introduce SplitShift (In Coworker Terms)
> *"SplitShift is a peer-to-peer web app built specifically for our crew. It lets us swap shifts in 2 clicks without spamming anyone."*
> 
> *"Here’s the best part: only coworkers who are actually free and trained for that station get notified. Nobody gets spammed with shifts they can’t work."*

### Step 3: Run the 3-Step Live Demo (Using Built-in Presentation Mode)
Click the **"Launch Staff Presentation"** button on the top banner of the app to show the visual slides, or walk them through live:

1. **Posting a Shift:**
   - Select **Leyla (Lead Barista)** from the top dropdown.
   - Click **"Request Cover"** on her Friday Morning shift (07:30 - 14:00).
   - Show how you can type a note: *"Got a midterm exam"*.
   - Tap **"Post Shift for Cover"**.

2. **Claiming Extra Hours (The Win-Win):**
   - Switch persona to **Murad (Barista)** in the top dropdown.
   - Open the **Shift Exchange Board**.
   - Show Murad: *"Murad wants extra cash for the weekend. He sees Leyla's shift, sees that he's 100% free and certified, and taps '✓ I'll Take This Shift (+6.5 hrs)'."*

3. **Official Manager Green Light:**
   - Switch to **Aysel (Manager)** or open the **Manager Desk** tab.
   - Show how the manager sees a green indicator: *"Zero Conflicts: Murad is certified and within safe weekly hours."*
   - Tap **"Approve Swap"**.
   - Show how the calendar immediately reassigns the shift to Murad, updates weekly hours, and sends approval alerts to both Leyla and Murad.

## 💡 4. Top 4 Staff Objections & How to Answer Them

| Staff Question / Worry | What You Say |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"Will our manager get mad if we swap shifts?"** | *"No, managers actually love this because it prevents last-minute call-outs and no-shows. Everything is transparent, so the manager has full peace of mind."* |
| **"Can someone steal my shifts without me knowing?"** | *"Never. A shift can only be picked up if YOU explicitly posted it to the board. And you can cancel your request anytime before someone claims it."* |
| **"What if I need extra cash this week?"** | *"Just open the Exchange Board. You'll see any open shifts your teammates need covered with the exact hours and estimated pay, and claim them in 1 tap."* |
| **"What if I don't want to get bothered with shift alerts on my days off?"** | *"Go to the 'My Availability' tab. Mark your university or personal days as Busy (Red). The system will never ping you for shifts on those days."* |

## 🛠️ 5. Built-in Features Tailored for Workers

- **Worker Persona Switcher:** Switch between 5 frontline staff (Leyla, Murad, Nigar, Rashad, Kamran) and Shift Lead Aysel to experience the app from any viewpoint.
- **Hours & Earnings Tracker:** Shows scheduled hours vs your personal target hours (e.g. 24h / 28h).
- **Intelligent Matchmaker:** Select any shift to instantly see which teammates are qualified, available, and have 1-click nudge buttons.
- **Weekly Availability Matrix:** Mon–Sun slot editor (Morning, Mid, Evening) that saves automatically.
- **Sound Feedback & Haptics:** Subtle audio cues on claiming, approving, and posting shifts.
- **Local Storage Persistence:** All edits and test swaps stay in your browser, with a 1-click "Reset All Demo Data" button.
