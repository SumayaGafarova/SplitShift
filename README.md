# SplitShift — Employee Shift Exchange Platform

SplitShift is a shift exchange and scheduling platform designed specifically for frontline employees in cafes, restaurants, and retail stores. It eliminates group chat chaos by enabling peer-to-peer shift giveaways, direct swaps, automated coworker eligibility matching, and manager sign-off.

## ✨ Key Features for Staff

1. **Staff-First Interactive Presentation Mode:** Built-in interactive slide deck (`Launch Staff Presentation`) explaining the pain points and benefits to cafe/retail coworkers.
2. **Weekly Shift Calendar:** Visual calendar with slot breakdowns (Morning, Mid, Evening), station certification tags, and one-click "Request Cover" or "Take Shift".
3. **Smart Coworker Matchmaker:** Instantly checks station qualification, schedule conflicts, and personal availability to show who can cover you without spamming the entire team.
4. **My Availability & Target Hours Tracker:** Frontline workers can mark when they are free or busy (classes, exams, family) and track weekly target hours.
5. **Manager Approval Desk:** Quick supervisor review with automated conflict and overtime detection.
6. **Mobile Simulation Mode:** One-click toggle between full desktop layout and simulated iPhone 15 frame.
7. **Simulated Personas:** Test from the perspective of Leyla (Lead Barista), Murad (Barista/Prep), Nigar (Host/Cashier), Rashad, Kamran, or Manager Aysel.
