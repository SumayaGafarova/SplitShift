Coworker Q&A: Your Questions Answered 💡

Q: Will management be mad if we swap shifts?

A: Quite the opposite! Managers hate sudden no-shows and scrambling to find replacements at 7:00 AM. When staff use SplitShift, shifts get covered reliably without the manager having to make 10 phone calls.

Q: Can someone take my shift without my permission?

A: Never. A shift is only open if YOU explicitly post it. If you change your mind before someone claims it, you can cancel the request with one click.

Q: What if no one picks up my shift?

A: SplitShift flags unassigned requests 24h prior and shows you exactly who is available so you can reach out directly.
